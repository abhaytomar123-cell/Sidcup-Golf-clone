ABOUT US

Home to a 46-bay, multi-tier, floodlit driving range, powered by Toptracer Range technology. Complimented by a practice green and bunker, coffee shop and American Golf Store. There truly is something for everyone as we also boast two outdoor 18-hole dinosaur themed crazy golf courses.

Please note:we are a cashless venue.

DRIVING RANGE

Our range delivers the same ball-tracking technology that traces the shots of the games's best players on TV.

State-of-the-art technology offers a fun,engaging,tech-driven experience that appeals to seasoned players,range rivals,friendly,family members,and even first-time golfers.
